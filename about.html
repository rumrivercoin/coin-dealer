About Rum River Coin

A Passion for Numismatics & Community

Serving collectors, investors, and families in Anoka, Minnesota since 2000. Honest appraisals, fair payouts, and a commitment to numismatic integrity.

Established in 2000

Guiding Minnesota Collectors for Over Two Decades

Rum River Coin was founded in 2000 in historic downtown Anoka with a straightforward vision: to create a trusted, collector-focused haven where anyone can buy, sell, or learn about rare coins and precious metals without the aggressive tactics of pawn shops or impersonal online brokers.

With more than 40 years of combined numismatic experience, our team has handled virtually every facet of the hobby—from early American copper cents and silver Morgan dollars to certified bullion bars, gold double eagles, and rare obsolete paper currency.

Located on 2nd Avenue just steps from the Rum River and easily accessible from Highway 10, our brick-and-mortar storefront welcomes visitors from Coon Rapids, Andover, Ramsey, Blaine, Champlin, and the greater Twin Cities metropolitan area.

Our Core Approach

Principles That Guide Every Transaction

Whether you are an experienced investor trading gold bullion or someone who just inherited a family coin collection, our values remain unwavering.

Ethical ANA Standards

As an active member of the American Numismatic Association (ANA), Rum River Coin upholds stringent ethical guidelines, strict counterfeit screening, and accurate grading criteria.

Welcoming & Non-Intimidating

We believe numismatics should be approachable. Jason and the team take the time to answer questions, share historical context, and help beginners understand their coins without jargon.

Live Stream Sales on Whatnot

In addition to our physical store, we host live stream coin and bullion auctions on Whatnot, allowing collectors across the nation to bid on certified coins and gold live.

Inherited Collections

Clear Insight for Inherited Coins & Valuables

Inheriting a coin collection can feel overwhelming. Many heirs don’t know where to start or worry about being taken advantage of by rapid-fire pawn shops.

At Rum River Coin, we sit down with you to review albums, mint sets, proof sets, and loose coins. We separate common circulation coins from valuable key dates, silver content, and collector pieces.

You will receive an honest appraisal detailing current market values, and if you choose to sell, we make an immediate, transparent cash or check offer on the spot.
