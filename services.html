High-Demand Bullion

Silver Bullion, Silver Eagles & Constitutional Silver

Silver offers tangible liquidity and strong collector demand. We maintain an active inventory of certified silver bars, sovereign government silver bullion coins, and historic 90% constitutional silver.

  • Silver Bullion Rounds & Bars: 1 oz, 5 oz, 10 oz, and 100 oz .999 fine silver bars (SilverTowne, Sunshine Mint, Johnson Matthey, Engelhard).
  • American Silver Eagles: Uncirculated rolls and individual dates from 1986 to present.
  • 90% Constitutional Silver: Pre-1965 dimes, quarters, and half-dollars traded by face value or bag.
  • 40% Silver Halves & War Nickels: Kennedy half dollars (1965–1970) and 35% silver war nickels (1942–1945).

Paper Money & Banknotes

Collectible Paper Currency & Banknotes

The history of American and world paper money is rich and diverse. Paper currency values depend on series date, signature combinations, bank issuing charter, seal colors, and condition.

  • Silver Certificates: Small and large-size $1, $5, and $10 blue seal notes.
  • United States Legal Tender Notes: Red seal $2 and $5 notes, historical portrait issues.
  • National Bank Notes: Rare currency issued by specific chartered local banks across Minnesota and the Midwest.
  • Gold Certificates & Obsolete Bills: Pre-Federal Reserve state and broken bank currency.

Family & Estate Service

Estate & Inherited Collection Appraisals

Handling an estate can be an emotionally taxing responsibility. When it comes to coin collections, you deserve an honest, knowledgeable, and patient guide who won't hurry you through the door.

At Rum River Coin, we conduct evaluations directly in our Anoka shop. We categorize items by collector rarity, silver/gold melt value, and general face value so you know exactly what each component is worth.

Estate Appraisal Tip:

Leave all coins in their original folders, envelopes, rolls, or albums. Never polish or clean coins, as cleaning destroys the original mint luster and dramatically reduces collector value.
