Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Rum River Coin, Anoka, MN

Website Owner Notice: This privacy policy reflects Rum River Coin's standard informational business website and contact form practices. Please review and update if additional automated tracking or third-party marketing tools are deployed.

Rum River Coin ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website (rumrivercoin.com), contact us by phone or email, or visit our physical coin shop at 2012 2nd Ave, Anoka, MN 55303.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide when interacting with us:

  • Inquiry & Contact Details: When you send a message through our contact form, call us, or email us, we collect your name, email address, telephone number, and the details regarding the coins, bullion, or estate collection you wish to discuss.
  • Transaction & Appraisal Records: In-person transactions involving precious metals, bullion, and coin purchases comply with state and local regulations for licensed bullion and secondhand dealers. Verification of government identification may be required in-store by law.
  • Technical & Log Data: Like most websites, our web hosting servers automatically record basic server log entries (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps) for security maintenance and operational stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries regarding rare coins, bullion prices, and collection appraisals.
  • To schedule and prepare for in-person evaluations at our Anoka storefront.
  • To comply with Minnesota statutory recordkeeping requirements governing precious metal transactions.
  • To maintain website security and prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website is designed to be informative and straightforward. We do not use intrusive third-party behavioral tracking cookies. Standard functional session cookies may be used by the web server to ensure pages load efficiently.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website contains links and embedded content from trusted third parties:

  • Google Maps: We embed an interactive Google Map to assist visitors in finding our shop in downtown Anoka. Google may collect anonymous device information subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Whatnot: We provide links to our official Whatnot live auction storefront. When you navigate to Whatnot, your interactions are governed by Whatnot’s terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement reasonable physical, technical, and organizational measures to safeguard any information you submit to us. Form submissions and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to address your requests or fulfill legal compliance requirements.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our website is directed to the general public and adult collectors. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
