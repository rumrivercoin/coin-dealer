About Rum River Coin

Experienced Numismatic Dealer Guiding Minnesota Collectors

Rum River Coin is a collector-focused coin and currency shop located in the historic heart of downtown Anoka, MN. We serve numismatists, investors, and families throughout Anoka County, Coon Rapids, Champlin, Andover, Ramsey, Blaine, and the northwest Twin Cities metro.

Whether you are looking to expand an established collection, liquidating inherited valuables, or trading precious metals, our numismatists evaluate every item with patience and transparency. We inspect your collection right in front of you—checking weight, metal purity, condition, rarity, and current live market demand.

As an active member of the American Numismatic Association (ANA), we adhere to the highest ethical grading standards. We take pride in creating a welcoming, non-intimidating environment for first-time visitors and veteran collectors alike.
